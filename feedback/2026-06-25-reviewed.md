## 2026-06-25 — knowledge-hub session (CISNET2)

Two frictions surfaced while pairing on a real task.

### 1 · Too many interruptions asking for my opinion

- Overall there were too many "what do you think?" gates. **The questions where
  the AI seemed to already have the answer were the annoying ones** — they felt
  like busywork, not engagement.
- Proposals (non-exclusive): reduce them overall, maybe with a **max count tied
  to the adversarial-depth decision**; offer an **option to disable**; **orient
  the questions better** (no concrete shape yet, but the AI-already-knows ones
  must go).

**Folded as:** new always-on directive §1.5 — *interrupt only where the human's
judgment has leverage; never gate busywork*. The depth gate (§2), freed up by
removing probes, now dials **interruption density** (`none/light/full`) and is
**re-dialable mid-session**. §4 reframed from "biased to interrupt" to "biased
to interrupt *where it has leverage*."

### 2 · The probes were not actually good — and biased my memory

- The planted "traps" weren't as good as intended and were sometimes
  **counter-productive: they lodged a biased memory** (a wrong recollection) even
  after being revealed.
- Verdict: **remove the idea from the skill.** For the same objective
  (engagement / retention), **post-process** mechanisms would be better:
  - A **rich, visual recap** — an explained tour of the modifications and
    architecture decisions. Before/after **diagrams with diff colours** are very
    good for memorisation.
  - **Collection of functional/conceptual novelties** — in the design/architecture
    of the systems fixed, or in how technologies were used. Exploitable later for
    **regular review, exercises (fine if a bit decorrelated), or summary**. Good
    for longer-term memory.

**Folded as:** §6 "Probes" deleted entirely (with its §1.5 quarantine directive,
§2 probe coupling, the Pause A/B probe lines, and the `probe`/`probe_caught`
fields in §7). Replaced by §6 "Retention (post-process, never planted)" — a
**visual before→after recap** and a **novelty ledger**
(`.personal/pair-with-me/novelties.md`), both run at Phase 6 Close. The decision
log (§8) records the continued-influence-effect rationale for the removal.
